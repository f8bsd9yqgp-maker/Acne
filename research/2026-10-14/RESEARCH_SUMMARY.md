# Acne susceptibility and durable-remission research — working summary
**Date:** 2026-10-14  
**Evidence status:** Hypothesis and literature synthesis. No compound, acne cure, tested formulation, human efficacy or clinical safety established.

## Core concept
Acne is produced by interacting follicular processes: local androgen/IGF-1 sensitivity, sebocyte lipid generation and peroxidation, ductal differentiation/comedogenesis, strain-specific *C. acnes* activity, innate immune signaling, and barrier condition. Similar blood hormone levels or bacterial abundance can produce different skin outcomes.

The proposed **follicular homeostasis** program tests, separately and in combinations, (a) healthier sebum lipid chemistry, (b) normal follicular keratinocyte differentiation, (c) extracellular microbial GehA-loop disruption, and (d) localized immune resolution *without* broad immune suppression. Interaction and durable remission are unproven.

## Interesting but unverified mechanistic priorities
- **GATA6 and ductal identity:** Reduced GATA6 has been observed in acne-associated follicular states. Does transient normalization improve plugging and remain normal after treatment withdrawal?
- **FABP5/CRABP2/retinoid routing:** Tissue observations implicate altered signaling. Expression correlations, pleiotropic AP-1 inhibitors and animal folliculitis models do not establish that FABP5 inhibition is safe or causal in human acne.
- **GehA:** A bacterial lipase implicated in lipid-dependent immune amplification; biochemical inhibition needs human skin efficacy, selectivity against host enzymes and microbiome-sparing evidence.
- **Lipid oxidation:** Protect physiological sebum and barrier lipids rather than aiming to remove all sebum.

## Computational result, with limits
A deterministic grid enumerated **102,631 control profiles** (NOT chemicals) and compared each on an assumed phenomenological model across 16 synthetic scenario combinations. Model-specific 'safety' guardrails retained 65,833 profiles. The nominal best mechanism family combined lipid-quality, ductal-identity, GehA neutralization and inflammation-resolution controls. Adjusting assumptions changes the family—emphasizing barrier support or a simpler two-target approach. The percentage improvements are **arbitrary model-proxy outputs, not measured or predicted clinical lesion reduction**.

## Falsifiable experimental program
1. Compare nonlesional, lesional and healthy follicles across independent human donors. Measure lipidomics, cell differentiation, GATA6/FABP5/CRABP2 signaling, strain-resolved GehA and inflammatory markers.
2. Use genetic perturbation/rescue controls and vehicle/standard-of-care comparators to separate causal target effects from expression associations.
3. Evaluate matched single-target and factorial-combination conditions. Assess microcomedones, cytokines, normal antibacterial functions, follicle anatomy and barrier health.
4. Verify intervention clears, then use ethically approved challenge and off-treatment follow-up. **Drug-free durable remission** must not be confused with on-treatment control.
5. Reject a candidate if it damages barrier/immune function, fails across independent donors, or relapses after washout.
